Hologram mission · voice channel diagnostic · 15 Aug 2026

What the microphone is losing

Four recordings measured end to end. The transcripts read as clean, continuous prose — and they are missing entire passages, disagreeing with themselves about laser wavelengths, and spelling Fedor's name five different ways.

The finding that matters most

Two transcription runs of the same twenty seconds of audio returned green 532 nm and green 530 nm. Nothing in either transcript indicates which one Fedor said.

This is the failure mode to design against. A garbled word announces itself — you read "Ray-Trade Stimulation" and know something went wrong. A wrong number does not. It arrives in fluent, well-punctuated English at a confidence score of 0.76, and it silently becomes a constraint in an optical design.

Isolating that moment to a 20-second clip and running it three times settled it: red 647 nm, green 532 nm, Verdi 5 W, identical all three times. Those are the real krypton and frequency-doubled Nd:YAG lines, so they are physically right. 530 appeared in one windowing of the same audio and not in others — which is the point. The number is not stable across runs, and nothing in the output tells you that.

!

Correction, same day

This report originally read the column below as a dose response to take length, and told Fedor to record in five-minute takes. That was wrong, and a peer session flagged it. My isolation test had changed two variables at once: when I cut a dropped window out and re-transcribed it, I also dropped the vocabulary flag.

Re-run properly on the same 11.5-minute file, with the vocabulary as the only variable:

wrong-domain vocabulary (64 terms)  104.3 s lost, 9 gaps  ·  no vocabulary  56.5 s lost, 5 gaps  ·  holography vocabulary (144 terms)  39.5 s lost, 4 gaps

The 32-second RGB-laser passage comes back in full at the original 11.5-minute length once the vocabulary is right. The loss was our misconfiguration, not his take length. The rule has been withdrawn from his handout.

Note also what this says about the mechanism: more hotwords gave the best coverage. It is not the count that breaks decoding, it is biasing toward terms that never occur in the audio.

The length column below still shows a real correlation, but length is now the confound rather than the cause — every one of these files was transcribed against the same wrong vocabulary, and a longer file simply contains more windows for it to damage:

Speech that was never written down

Every silence longer than three seconds was cross-checked against waveform energy, then the largest gaps were cut out and re-transcribed alone. One 32-second window in display-ru contains this, and it appears nowhere in the finished transcript:

“ты мне просто дай, как лучи будут распространяться. Во-первых, во время записи, когда мы используем красный, синий, зелёный лазер с нужными длин[ами волн]. И [во] время воспроизведения…”

The proof that it is absent rather than relocated: красн, син and зелен each return zero hits across the full 655-word transcript. The RGB recording-wavelength explanation — a hard design constraint — is simply gone, with no marker where it used to be.

In hologram-2 the same defect appears twice as holes of exactly 30.0 seconds. That is precisely Whisper's decode window, which tells us the mechanism: on long files the decoder occasionally emits nothing at all for one entire chunk. Short takes do not do this.

Three causes, and only two are Fedor's

Sorting every weak word by cause changes who has to act. The largest single category is not pronunciation at all.

Ours to fix — configuration

The vocabulary file loaded for every run was 81 lines of Decentrix, Databricks, Snowflake and DISH. Zero holography terms. Fedor's name absent. He can articulate perfectly and still lose every one of these.

Fedor's to fix — take structure

Length of take, which language he opens in, whether numbers are spoken once or twice, how far the microphone sits, and whether anyone else is talking in the room.

What Fedor should change, in order

Ranked by how much content each one saves, divided by how much effort it costs him. The first two recover material that is otherwise lost completely; the rest improve material that merely arrives damaged.

1

Record in takes of about five minutes

Withdrawn. The content loss this rule was built on came from the wrong vocabulary file, not from take length — see the correction above. He can keep recording at whatever length suits his thinking.

1

Say numbers twice, and write them down as well

“Green, five three two nanometres — 532.” Then put the wavelengths, powers and dimensions in a text message or a photo of a notebook page.

The numbers are the one thing voice cannot be trusted with. Two runs of the same audio returned 532 and 530. Eleven of forty numbers in hologram-2 came back uncertain, including "4x4" at confidence 0.16. The mirror is also described as 45×50 inches in one place and 45×55 in another — unresolved, and it needs to be.
2

Don't put a Russian lead-in on an English recording

This one is Dmitry's, not Fedor's. Record the framing comment as its own separate file.

hologram-2 is 97% English by word count, but the opening “Продолжай” caused Whisper to tag the whole file ru and decode fourteen minutes of English through a Russian-primed decoder. The wreckage lands on the laser specs: eigentlich, В fait, Boxes R, coagulated for “coherent”.
3

Move the microphone to about 20 cm

Closer mic, quieter room, door shut, fan off. A one-time fix with no ongoing effort.

Consonant energy above 4 kHz measured 2.9% in hologram-2 and 6.7% in display-ru; healthy close-mic speech runs above 12%. Consonants are exactly what separates similar words. display-ru also peaked at 0.0 dBFS with 18 clipped samples over a 24 dB noise margin.
4

Spell a product name once, the first time you use it

“Zemax — Z, E, M, A, X.” Once per recording is enough.

Zemax produced Зимокс, Дзимокс, ZIMAX, Vimux, ZMAX and Друзья и Макс. It is the one term that resisted the vocabulary fix entirely — biasing both Latin and Cyrillic spellings in did not recover it.
5

Pause the recording for side conversations

If someone comes into the room, stop the take.

A domestic exchange — “пойдем сюда, нам некогда… просто завтраку” — sits in the middle of the mission transcript, formatted exactly like engineering content.

The loop that keeps this from drifting back

Advice decays. A check does not. Two commands, both running fully on-device, so Fedor gets a verdict without anyone reading a transcript.

Before recording — 30 seconds

fedor-check mic clip.m4a

Scores level, headroom, room noise, consonant energy and bandwidth, then prints GO, USABLE or FIX FIRST. Catches a bad mic position before twenty minutes are spent on it, which is the whole point — the room is the one problem you cannot repair afterwards.

After recording

fedor-check take <dir>

Five measures — take length, dropped speech, language, numbers at risk, room — each with a threshold drawn from his own recordings. It ends by naming one thing to change next time, not six.

The thresholds are not general advice about diction. Every one was set from a measurement of Fedor's own audio, and the tool says which recording each came from. When a new term comes back mangled it goes into the vocabulary the same day, with the mangling recorded next to it — that is what keeps the fix from rotting.

Fixed on our side, no action needed from Fedor

  • A holography vocabulary in Russian and English — 180 terms, each annotated with the actual corruption that motivated it, including every English term he code-switches into mid-sentence.
  • A deterministic glossary for the terms that biasing cannot reach, so Друзья и Макс becomes Zemax by rewrite rather than by luck.
  • Fedor's name, in Latin and Cyrillic, so it stops arriving as an unknown instruction.
  • Ambiguities deliberately left alone rather than auto-corrected: операции is almost certainly аберрации, but it is a real word, so it is flagged for a human instead of silently rewritten.

Still open — verify against the audio before building on it

  • Mirror dimensions. 45×50 inches and 45×55 inches both appear. One is wrong.
  • Exposure figure. "4x4 time" at confidence 0.16, in the middle of the exposure-time discussion. Unrecovered.
  • Vehicle type. "бирусчивого транспорта" — unrecovered. Worth asking rather than guessing.
  • The 32 dropped seconds should be re-transcribed as a clip and folded back into the record, since the RGB recording wavelengths are stated there and nowhere else.
  • Hologram 1 (22.6 min) was still transcribing when this was written, against the wrong vocabulary and at a length that has dropped content in every other file.
