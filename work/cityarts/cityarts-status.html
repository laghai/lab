Nothing you do can be lost

Every version of every page is kept, permanently, in two places at once: your own laptop and an archive off the premises. Nothing is ever overwritten and nothing is ever thrown away — a change adds a new version beside the old one rather than replacing it.

Any earlier state can be brought back exactly as it was, including work you have already shown to someone. Undoing is one sentence. This is the part you can stop worrying about permanently.

Everyone is looking at the same thing

Three states exist and they never blur. What is live is what a visitor would see. What is approved is the version Dmitry has signed off. What is in progress is yours, and stays private until you decide to show it.

The three cards at the top of this page were read from the live addresses a moment ago. If you and Dmitry ever seem to be discussing different pages, those cards settle it — as they did today.

Two people cannot overwrite each other

Everyone works at their own desk on their own copy. Two people can work the same week on the same page and neither can touch the other’s draft.

If two people do change the same paragraph, the system stops and asks. It will not guess a winner and it will not quietly discard one. Someone reads both and decides — that refusal is the protection, and it is why nothing you did this week had to be reconciled with anything.
