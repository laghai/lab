Three steps, about ninety seconds

The brain restores itself the moment you log in — that part is already done and does not wait on you. These are the two things a machine may not do on your behalf, plus the command that starts a session.

  1. brainStarts or reattaches a session inside tmux. Closing the tab does not end the work — that is the entire point of moving it here.
  2. /loginOne browser prompt, once. An unauthenticated Claude on that box answers “Not logged in · Please run /login” — I probed it, so this is what you will actually see.
  3. brain-bootstrapOptional, when you want the code: a GitHub device flow, then all 14 repos clone into ~/projects.

What is not done

  • AWS. You said “actually try both.” The AWS lane is blocked on one access key — about two minutes of your time. Nothing here is GCP-specific except the provisioning verb.
  • The return path. You can reach the brain. The brain reaching you — Gmail, Google Chat, iMessage — is a separate track already in flight on brain/feat/return-path.
  • The memo inbox is still Mac-only. This is the single biggest remaining laptop dependency. You already named the fix in the memo: drop memos into Google Drive the way you do at work.
  • cloudup’s VM verbs are broken — found by the cloud-team track, reproduced here. up, ls, ssh, down all return UNAVAILABLE blaming “no credentialed provider yet” on the line after doctor proves GCP is credentialed. It is a function-naming mismatch, not a credential gap, and the error sends you to fix the wrong thing. cloudbrain is unaffected.
  • The six scheduled jobs are launchd. They need systemd timers on Linux. The intervals are the tuned part and they port directly.
